# Evals

## Score Scale

- 1: unusable
- 2: weak
- 3: acceptable but needs cleanup
- 4: strong
- 5: publish-ready

## Idea Eval

Dimensions:
- relatability
- shareability
- comment potential
- visual clarity
- emotional truth
- humor/warmth balance
- freshness
- brand fit
- simplicity
- carousel payoff

Selection threshold: `4.0`.

## Pre-Generation Eval

Dimensions:
- story coherence
- slide count fit
- scene selection quality
- visual clarity
- prompt clarity
- character consistency potential
- style consistency potential
- text readability potential
- brand fit
- publishability
- risk of generic output
- risk of visual failure

Revision threshold: below `4.0`.

## Image Eval

Dimensions:
- identity match
- face consistency across slides
- style consistency
- text readability
- exact text preservation
- brandmark presence
- neutral ivory paper
- scene logic
- pose/anatomy
- composition
- publishability

Image retry threshold: below `3.8`, or any hard failure.
