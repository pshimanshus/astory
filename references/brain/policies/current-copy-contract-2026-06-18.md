# Current Copy Contract

Status: active policy.

Use before writing A Story of Two copy, captions, hooks, carousel text, scene
directions, prompt copy, or creative recommendations.

- Every line is written as a sentence someone could send, not a content pillar.
- The source-bank forms stay visible, but they are not treated as templates to fill.
- Visual wrappers are restrained; they prove the feeling without becoming quirky behavior.
- No chappal, dhaniya, roti, spoon, or object-led invention leads the premise.
- The copy aims for "I needed to say this" before "this is a carousel."

Operational meaning:

- Preserve the emotional engine of winning sources without reducing them to fill-in structures.
- Start from a human need to send the line, not from a carousel architecture.
- Reject object-led cleverness when the object becomes the premise instead of serving the feeling.
- Keep visuals quiet enough that the line and emotional situation remain the reason to share.
