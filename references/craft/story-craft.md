# Story Craft — A Story of Two

Distilled application of Save the Cat (beats), Story Grid (love genre), Pixar /
story-circle, and Every Frame a Painting (visual storytelling). This is *derived
craft* in our voice — not reproduced source text. It fills what `story-canon/`
indexed but never wrote down.

## The one equation under every A Story post

> A story exists when someone **wants something they can't quite say**, and a
> **turn** makes the unsaid visible.

- **desire** (the unsaid feeling) + **tension/obstacle** + **a turn** = a story.
- No turn → it's a quote. No unsaid → it's a caption. No concrete receipt → it's an
  essay. The "mathematics": *setup → tension → turn → the unsaid lands.*

## Two shapes

### Single illustration (most of your strongest)
One frame holds the whole arc:
- the **line** carries the desire/turn (the reframe: *"not asking = caring"*);
- the **frame** carries the proof (the receipt: the unasked chai);
- the **gap** between what's said and what's shown *is* the story. She says "I'm
  fine," the image shows she isn't, and he already knows. (Every Frame a Painting:
  let the picture say what the words won't.)

### Multi (2–10): the swipe is the engine
- **Slide 1 = hook**: a curiosity gap or a claim that demands the next slide
  (*"He didn't marry peace"*).
- **Middle = escalating specific receipts**, each a fresh scene/turn, never a
  restated point. (Save the Cat: every beat raises stakes; no repeats.)
- **Final = the turn home**: reframe the whole, land the unsaid, earn the send —
  never explain the lesson.
- **Length is chosen by how many true receipts you have.** Three real receipts beat
  six padded ones.

## Love-genre obligatory moments (Story Grid), at couple scale
An A Story post is a micro love-scene. Stage **one** of these per post — never the
whole arc:
- the lovers truly **see** each other (*"reads the 'I'm fine' you don't mean"*);
- a **proof of love** that costs something small but real (he holds her shoes; she
  waits up);
- the **gap that almost wins** (a fight, a silence, pride) and the quiet repair;
- the **private language** only they have (the inside joke; *"you are my yellow"*).

## Hook craft (not gimmick)
Earns the first second by answering: *do I care? does this feel newly seen? do I
want the next beat?* Valid hooks: curiosity gap; direct address (*"Be with someone
who…"*); a true confession (*"in a world of billions, are you the one I found?"*).
Never fake secrets, "nobody talks about this," or hype.

## Visual storytelling (the frame is an actor)
- **blocking carries emotion**: who leans in, who turns away, the unclaimed space;
- **one prop does the narrative work** (the held shoes, the chai, the pillow) — cut
  the rest;
- the **body must prove the line**: eyeline, posture, gesture cannot contradict the
  text;
- **vary the frame** — not the default sideways two-face close-up; go wide /
  over-the-shoulder / action when the story allows.

## The anti-pattern guardrail
If you can describe the post as *"the [X] format,"* you're templating → monotony.
**Test:** would this still move someone who's seen 50 A Story posts this week? If it
feels like a formula they've already felt, find the fresher true thing.
