## Two lanes (don't confuse them)

Reading all 87 posts end-to-end, your account runs **two distinct content lines** —
keep them straight, because they win differently:

- **Illustrated-story** (~18 carousels) — the **product**: an Aachu/Zuv illustrated
  scene that turns a true feeling. This is the soul lane and where the studio
  competes. Wins **saves** (keep-worthy) and **sendable shares** when the turn is
  comic. *(Everything in engines A–F below.)*
- **Real-couple-reel** (~69 videos) — your real-couple **comedy/romance** line. It
  drives your **biggest reach** (3.2M, 2.9M, 2.1M) and share-rate (up to 80/1k) on
  sharp desi-marriage observations. It is **not** the illustrated product — but it is
  the truest evidence of your *voice*: Hinglish, specific, funny, never poetic.

**Generation target = the illustrated lane.** But the comedy lane's voice (engines
G–H) is your antidote to the "AI mood-portrait" flop register — when an illustrated
draft drifts soft/literary, pull it back toward how the reels actually talk.

## Cross-cutting truths (what separates winners from flops)

1. **Two people interacting** (winners) vs **one person alone** (flops).
2. **A turn/reframe** vs a flat pretty statement.
3. **A specific receipt** — socks-before-pants, the held juttis, the chai, the
   pillow — vs a vague claim.
4. **Authentic Hinglish character-lines** ("main kar lungi", "mujhe kuch nahi hua")
   are gold; render them romanized.
5. **Range of looks** — bright wedding, dark candlelit, sunset mountain, street
   market. House style = finish/quality, **not** a fixed composition.
6. **Shares vs saves are different jobs:** comedic *loving-complaint* → shares
   (send to partner); *observational/aspirational* intimacy → saves (keep it). Decide
   which behavior you're going for before you write.
