# Style References

Approved A Story of Two style references live here.

## Approved Bundles

- `observational-intimacy-premium/`: primary approved style lock, imported 2026-06-07.

The desired look is premium hand-drawn romantic watercolor-and-ink illustration on neutral ivory/off-white paper with visible grain, fine ink/pencil linework, transparent watercolor blooms, tactile details, integrated readable handwritten text, and a tiny `@a.storyof.two` brandmark.

Avoid yellow/parchment paper, generic AI watercolor, quote-card design, anime, photorealism, 3D render, and flat vector art.
