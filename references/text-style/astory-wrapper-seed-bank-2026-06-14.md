## Strongest Direction

The best next batch should not be "quotes illustrated by couples." It should be
objects behaving like feelings:

- water bottle as apology
- charger as bridge
- plate as loyalty
- blanket as accusation
- phone glow as longing
- cupboard space as permanence
- tea cup as truce
- door latch as waiting

If the object can tell the story without the line, the line can stay small.
