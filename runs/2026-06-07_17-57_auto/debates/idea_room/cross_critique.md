## Relatability Critique

The battery idea is the most immediately recognizable micro-behavior, but the phone-specific proof can pull the slide toward a tiny UI detail. The shawl, tea, errand, and hair-tie variants hold the same emotional truth with more tactile props and less risk of unreadable device text.

The food-sharing cluster is warm and easy to tag, but it has a visible `GENERIC_IDEA` risk unless the final bite is extremely specific. It is viable as a backup, not the strongest flagship idea.

## Shareability Critique

`He Heard The Quiet Part` has the best private-send hook because it lets viewers map their own relationship habit onto the story. Its risk is abstraction: if the story stays general, the carousel may need too much caption explanation. It needs a single hero prop that proves the hook by slide 2.

## Visual Critique

`The Shawl He Already Brought` has the cleanest production path. The shawl is large, readable, recurring, emotionally warm, and composable inside the house style. It gives the prompt room a clear prop anchor and avoids tiny phone, food, or UI details.

Risk: draping fabric around bodies can create awkward hands or arm geometry. The repair should favor holding, offering, placing over shoulders, or shared-cover poses over complex wrapping.

## Decision

The best repaired direction is not a pure pick from one agent. It combines:

- The emotional hook from `He Heard The Quiet Part`.
- The observed practical-care truth from `The 3% Battery Rescue`.
- The visual prop and slide safety from `The Shawl He Already Brought`.
