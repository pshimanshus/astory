# Story Room Debate

Run: `2026-06-07_17-57_auto`

Approved idea: `The Shawl He Brought Before She Asked`

## Story Director Pass

The story should stay small and observational. The emotional engine is not "Zuv saves Aachu from cold"; it is "Aachu is known before she has to explain." The shawl should appear as practical evidence, not a romantic trophy.

Recommended arc:

1. Hook: he answered something unspoken.
2. Setup: she is doing the familiar "I'm fine" act.
3. Reveal: he quietly brings the shawl without requiring confession.
4. Payoff: being noticed is warmer than the object itself.

## Pacing Editor Pass

Four slides are necessary and sufficient. Three slides compress the denial and reveal too tightly, creating `TOO_FEW_SLIDES` and `WEAK_PAYOFF` risk. Five or more slides would repeat the breeze or over-ceremonialize the shawl, creating `TOO_MANY_SLIDES` and `GENERIC_IDEA` risk.

Protected slides:

- Slide 1 for share hook.
- Slide 2 for Aachu's agency and humor.
- Slide 3 for visual proof.
- Slide 4 for emotional payoff.

## Swipe Retention Critic Pass

The first slide has strong send-to-someone energy because it starts with the share hook. Slide 2 adds curiosity by showing what the unspoken sentence was. Slide 3 gives the satisfying concrete reveal. Slide 4 must avoid turning into a generic quote, so the shawl remains visible and the text stays specific.

Retention risk: if slide 3 already wraps the shawl fully, slide 4 has nowhere to go. Keep slide 3 as the offer and slide 4 as the settled warmth.
