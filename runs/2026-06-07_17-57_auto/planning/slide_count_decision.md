## Recommended Count

`4`

## Why This Count Serves The Story

Four slides gives the idea a clean emotional shape: immediate share hook, Aachu's breezy denial, Zuv's quiet evidence of noticing, and a concrete payoff that stays attached to the shawl. Each slide changes the viewer's understanding without stretching a one-object moment into filler.

## Why Fewer Slides Would Fail

Three slides would force the hook, denial, shawl reveal, and relationship truth to compete. The story would likely skip either Aachu's self-contained "I'm fine" act or the emotional distinction between rescue and being noticed, creating `TOO_FEW_SLIDES` and possible `WEAK_PAYOFF` risk.

## Why More Slides Would Dilute

Five or more slides would over-explain a small, observational moment. Extra beats would likely repeat the breeze, make Zuv too heroic, or turn the ending into a generic tenderness quote, creating `TOO_MANY_SLIDES` and `GENERIC_IDEA` risk.

## Slides Protected

- Slide 1: share hook and premise.
- Slide 2: Aachu's self-contained denial, so she is not helpless.
- Slide 3: Zuv's quiet action, so the care is proven visually.
- Slide 4: payoff anchored in the shawl, not a detached quote-card.
