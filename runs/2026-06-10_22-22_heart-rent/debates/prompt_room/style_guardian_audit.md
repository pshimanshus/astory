# Style Guardian Audit

Status: fail current candidate; setup repair required.

## Root Cause

The candidate is too literal and staged. The wallet, readable note, red paper heart, hand-on-chest pose, and smiling close two-shot make the image feel like a performed skit. The premium references work because they feel observed: ordinary rooms, natural body language, small unforced details, airy paper, and quiet restraint.

## Better Setup

Use a lived-in doorway or bedroom threshold moment. Aachu is slightly left/lower-left, half-smiling with mock seriousness, holding a tiny blank rent slip or small wallet casually at waist/chest height. Zuv is right/lower-right, mid-action as if arriving or leaving, with keys, tote, jacket, or one hand near pocket, smiling down at her like he has been caught living there emotionally.

## Composition And Style

- Native 4:5 portrait.
- Couple in the lower half with breathing room and ordinary environment around them.
- Text in clean upper-middle negative space:

```text
pay rent for living in
my heart
```

- No prop text.
- Warm charcoal hand-drawn typography baked into the paper.
- Neutral ivory/off-white paper.
- Soft navy, denim, off-white cotton, muted grey, faint peach/blush accents.
- Transparent watercolor washes, fine pencil/ink contours, delicate crosshatching, visible paper grain, soft faded edges.

## Hard Rejects

- Oversized wallet.
- Readable `heart rent due` note.
- Red paper heart as the main prop.
- Hand-on-heart theatrical pose.
- Heart cushion or wall nook.
- Quote-card feeling.
- Generic AI couple beauty.
- Crowded couch tableau.
- Yellow parchment paper.
- Extra words, labels, UI, or decorative clutter.
