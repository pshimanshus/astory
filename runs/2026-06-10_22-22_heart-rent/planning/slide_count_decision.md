# Slide Count Decision

Recommended slide count: 1.

## Why One Slide Wins

The exact text `pay rent for living in my heart` is a one-beat joke. It should land immediately as a premium illustrated meme-adaptation.

## Why Fewer Fails

Fewer than one slide is impossible.

## Why More Dilutes

Two or more slides would stretch a simple hook into setup/explanation. The audience does not need narrative buildup to understand the joke. Extra slides would increase identity drift risk and reduce the meme-like immediacy.

## Output Surfaces After Approval

- Native 4:5 Instagram post.
- Separate native 9:16 Reels/Stories version.

Do not resize one surface into the other.
