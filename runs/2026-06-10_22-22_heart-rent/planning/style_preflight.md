House style:

- premium hand-drawn romantic editorial watercolor-and-ink illustration
- warm neutral ivory/off-white paper, never yellow or parchment
- visible paper grain and delicate ink/pencil linework
- soft watercolor blooms and faded organic edges
- intimate everyday couple scene
- airy upper-middle negative space for readable hand-drawn text
- tiny low-contrast handwritten `@a.storyof.two` bottom-right

Text style:

- exact wording: `pay rent for living in my heart`
- warm charcoal handwritten typography
- readable at phone-screen size
- baked into the illustration, not a digital overlay

Style risks:

- `TEXT_NOT_EXACT` if the model changes line order or punctuation.
- `YELLOW_PAPER_CAST` if paper becomes parchment.
- `GENERIC_IDEA` if the result becomes a quote card instead of a lived-in scene.
