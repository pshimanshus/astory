# Idea Room Cross Critique

## Relatability Ethnographer

The source jokes are already strong because they are not grand romance; they are daily couple behavior. The risk is making them too polished and losing the slightly chaotic teasing. Keep the images casual and lived-in.

## Shareability Strategist

Both lines are taggable. The first works if the phone/reel evidence is visible. The second works if Aachu looks proud of being the "annoyance" and Zuv looks secretly happy, not genuinely irritated.

## Visual Story Director

Do not literally recreate the cat squeeze as choking, restraint, or animal mimicry. Translate it into a safe cuddle trap. For the heart meme, keep the dusty coral heart idea but avoid making the asset a quote-card or centered poster.
