# Visual Composition Director Audit

Inspected: rejected exports `slide_01_4x5`, `slide_01_9x16`, `slide_02_4x5`, `slide_02_9x16`; source meme screenshots `source_01_video-watch.jpeg`, `source_02_annoy-you.jpeg`; style anchors `observational-intimacy-premium/contact-sheet.png`, `slide-01.png`, `slide-03.png`, `slide-07.png`; Aachu identity/body-language refs.

## 1. Composition Failures

- Slide 01 became a static sofa cuddle portrait, not a funny "watch my videos" couple threat. Aachu is pinned behind Zuv and absorbed into the couch, so her body language reads cramped instead of playfully dominant.
- Aachu's face is too low-energy and over-rendered: heavier cheeks, older expression, and compressed jaw/neck from the hug angle. The scarf/arms crowd her face and make the identity read muddy at thumbnail size.
- Zuv dominates the frame mass and expression. The phone helps the concept, but the physical comedy from the cat meme is softened into polite coziness.
- Slide 02 has the same couch/desk compression problem. Aachu is partly hidden behind the heart and behind Zuv, while Zuv takes the foreground, so the "I annoy you" joke is not owned by her.
- The scene setting is too busy with laptop/book/mug/decor. These props pull attention away from the meme beat and make the image feel staged, not chaotic-couple shareable.
- Both slides drift from the approved style lock by overfilling the lower frame and underusing clean negative space. The style references work best when one clear everyday action carries the frame, with breathable text space above.

## 2. Revised Scene Staging

### Slide 01: "me if you don't watch the videos I send you."

- Stage it as a playful ambush, not a sofa squeeze. Put Zuv seated on the floor or sofa edge, slightly forward, holding his phone toward camera with obvious unwatched video cards.
- Place Aachu clearly behind and above him, but not buried: one knee on the sofa edge or standing behind the sofa arm, torso visible from waist up, face fully unobstructed.
- Her pose: mischievous deadpan/pout, one eyebrow raised, arms looped loosely around his shoulders with a scarf like a comic "you cannot escape" wrap. No neck choke, no face squashing.
- His pose: laughing side-eye or mock surrender, one hand up, shoulders tilted away from her. The couple energy should read as "she is lovingly impossible, he knows it."
- Compose as a diagonal: Aachu upper-left/center, Zuv lower-right/center, phone lower-left/center. Keep the room simple: one sofa, one plant, one cup, one notebook.

### Slide 02: "imagine how boring your life would be if you didn't have me to annoy you"

- Make Aachu the clear owner of the joke. Put her front-left or center-left, holding a handmade paper heart frame around her own face, shoulders and hands fully visible.
- Zuv should sit/lean to the right, interrupted mid-reading or mid-laptop, turning toward her with fond disbelief. He should not block her or become the main mass.
- The heart prop should feel handmade and chaotic: taped edges, doodled mini hearts, uneven paper, maybe loose scraps on the table. It must frame Aachu's face without hiding cheeks, chin, hairline, or hands.
- Show active banter: Aachu peeking through the heart with a smug grin; Zuv half-smiling, half-exasperated, caught in the exact moment she invades his calm.
- Reduce props to only what supports the joke: paper heart scraps, tape, one mug, phone with tiny heart stickers. Avoid a large laptop dominating the bottom-right.

## 3. Framing And Pose Rules

- Aachu must be clearly readable first or equal-first in both slides. Minimum: full face visible, both eyes visible, chin visible, at least one shoulder/arm visible, and no sofa cushion/prop swallowing her silhouette.
- At thumbnail size, viewers must read three things in one second: Aachu's playful attack, Zuv's amused surrender, and the meme text.
- Keep both faces large and clean: each face should occupy roughly 12-18% of frame width in 4:5, with Aachu never smaller than Zuv by more than 10%.
- Avoid front-facing lens compression and cramped cushion poses that widen the face. Use gentle 3/4 angles, visible neck/shoulder separation, loose hair framing, and lighter under-eye/cheek rendering for Aachu.
- Preserve Aachu's youthful identity: soft oval face, bright eyes, natural brows, long dark hair, fresh expression. Reject any version that makes her look older, puffy, stern, generic, or hidden.
- Use one strong action silhouette per slide. If the action is not readable as a black-and-white thumbnail, the composition is not ready.
- Maintain the A Story of Two design aesthetic: ivory paper, airy top text, premium watercolor-and-ink linework, soft everyday room details, tiny low-contrast brandmark.

## 4. Hard Rejection Criteria

- Reject if Aachu is tucked into a sofa, hidden behind Zuv, hidden behind a heart, cropped at the face, or visually secondary.
- Reject if either face looks heavier, older, generic, over-beautified, or meaningfully different from identity references.
- Reject if the joke reads as calm romance instead of affectionate chaos and banter.
- Reject if Zuv's body, laptop, book, scarf, heart, or phone blocks Aachu's face/neck/shoulders.
- Reject if the composition relies on clutter instead of one clear meme action.
- Reject if text is not exact, not legible, or competes with faces.
- Reject if screenshot UI, animals, photorealism, anime/cartoon styling, flat vector styling, or digital overlay typography appears.
- Reject if the image fails the one-second thumbnail test: Aachu visible, couple dynamic visible, joke visible.
