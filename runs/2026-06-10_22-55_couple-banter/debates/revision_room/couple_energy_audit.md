# Couple Energy and Shareability Audit

Role: Couple Energy and Shareability Critic

## 1. Why the Current Images Feel Too Static and Safe

- The meme conflict got softened into a polished couch portrait. Slide 01 should feel like "watch my reels or suffer affectionate consequences," but the current pose reads like a calm scarf cuddle. No real squeeze, no comic pressure, no captured-in-the-act chaos.
- Aachu is visually secondary in both slides. She is tucked into the sofa/background, partially hidden by props, and her expression is too still. The creator's complaint is correct: she needs to be young, clear, mischievous, and immediately readable.
- Faces are over-rounded and aged by painterly softness. Both leads look heavier in the face than the references, and Aachu especially loses her fresh, playful identity.
- Slide 02 misses the joke mechanism. The source meme works because the heart prop is absurdly direct and the character is shamelessly annoying. The current version makes Aachu sweet and decorative instead of proudly intrusive.
- The scenes are too cozy and arranged. Plants, lamps, notebooks, and neat props create "romantic lifestyle illustration" energy, while the ask needs couple banter: playful attack, interruption, teasing, and lovable nuisance behavior.
- The compositions do not have a fast social hook. A viewer should understand each joke in under one second from the body language alone, before reading the text.

## 2. Sharable Visual Hooks

### Slide 01: "me if you don't watch the videos I send you."

- Replace the soft scarf cuddle with a clear playful headlock/hug trap. Aachu should be behind Zuv, fully visible, leaning over his shoulder with a mock-serious "I warned you" face.
- Zuv should be laughing, half-squished, slightly trapped, one hand raised in surrender, the other holding a phone with obvious unwatched video bubbles/play icons.
- Make the composition tighter and more meme-like: faces large, bodies diagonally tilted, scarf/dupatta/arms forming the visual "trap" around him.
- Add a readable reaction contrast: Aachu deadpan possessive menace, Zuv amused panic. That contrast is the share trigger.
- The text should feel like the punchline floating over a captured moment, not like a caption on a posed portrait.

### Slide 02: "imagine how boring your life would be if you didn't have me to annoy you"

- Aachu must be the lovable interruption. Put her closer to camera, holding a big handmade heart frame around her face, photobombing Zuv's calm activity.
- Zuv should look interrupted but fond: trying to read/work/drink chai while side-eyeing her with a smile that says "this is my daily problem."
- The heart prop should be intentionally silly: uneven paper edges, tape, tiny doodles, maybe slightly too big for the space. It should read as a gag, not decor.
- Use a stronger overlap: Aachu invading Zuv's personal space from behind/side, her face clear through the heart, his posture leaning away but emotionally pulled in.
- The share hook is "annoying but impossible to live without"; make it visible through action, not just text.

## 3. Small Chaotic/Lived-In Details That Do Not Clutter

- Phone screen with 3-5 sent video thumbnails and one tiny "seen?" or unread dot cue.
- One fallen cushion or rumpled throw showing the playful struggle just happened.
- Chai cup near the edge, not spilled, adding tension without mess.
- A tiny sticky note or scrap heart that says "watch it" / "annoying dept." only if legible; otherwise keep it as a doodle.
- Slightly crooked heart cutout, visible tape, handmade fold line.
- One open book/laptop/remote as Zuv's interrupted activity, but keep it secondary to faces and gesture.
- Hair motion and loose scarf/dupatta movement to add energy without adding more objects.
- Keep background minimal: warm wall, sofa/bed edge, one plant or lamp maximum. The couple and joke must own the frame.

## 4. Hard Rejection Criteria for the Revision

- Reject if Aachu is hidden, cropped awkwardly, stuffed into the sofa, or not instantly readable.
- Reject if either face appears older, heavier/rounder, generic, merged, over-beautified, or inconsistent with the supplied references.
- Reject if the body language reads as calm posing instead of active teasing/interruption.
- Reject if Slide 01 lacks a clear playful trap/headlock/surrender dynamic.
- Reject if Slide 02 lacks a shamelessly silly heart-frame interruption dynamic.
- Reject if the image depends on room decor to feel complete; the joke must work even with the background ignored.
- Reject if the composition has low meme readability at thumbnail size.
- Reject if text is wrong, missing, cramped, or visually competing with faces.
- Reject if the scene becomes mean, aggressive, or uncomfortable. The chaos must remain affectionate, consensual, and couple-coded.
- Reject if the final looks like a generic romantic watercolor portrait instead of a sharable A Story of Two banter moment.
