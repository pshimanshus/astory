# Story Room Debate

## Story Director

The set should feel like two tiny truths from the same relationship. The first is mock consequence: "watch what I send you." The second is mock justification: "your life needs my noise." Both should be funny because the affection is obvious.

## Pacing Editor

Two standalone assets are enough. The viewer gets one instant joke per image. Do not add extra story panels.

## Swipe Retention Critic

The first image needs a strong phone cue. The second needs Zuv's amused face so the word "annoy" does not become negative.

## Locked Direction

- Slide 1: sofa cuddle trap, phone with unwatched video cues.
- Slide 2: handmade dusty coral heart prop, Aachu as proud favorite interruption.
