# Slide Count Decision

Decision: two standalone illustrations.

Why two:

- The creator attached two separate meme sources with two separate exact texts.
- Each joke needs its own visual proof and emotional beat.
- The pair can work as two standalone posts or as a light mini-set.

Why fewer fails:

- One combined illustration would force both texts into one scene and dilute both jokes.

Why more dilutes:

- A multi-slide carousel would add setup that the source memes do not need. The humor is immediate.

Surfaces:

- Native 4:5 Instagram post for each illustration.
- Native 9:16 story/reel version for each illustration.
