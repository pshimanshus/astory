# Idea Engagement Report

Selected idea: `Promise, Proved In Three Rooms`

Status: pass at `4.78 / 5.0`

## Why It Should Travel

The promise starts as one small private gesture, then gets proved through ordinary care. People can send it to the partner who shows up during a happy mess and after a hard evening without needing a big speech.

## Why It Fits A Story Of Two

It refuses a generic promise poster. The emotion comes from objects, posture, practical care, and quiet hand-near-hand presence instead of contorted symbolic posing.

## Main Risk

If the scenes collapse into close-up hands with text, the idea fails. Every post must include enough Aachu/Zuv context to feel observed, not borrowed. Forced linked-pinky posing after Post 1 is rejected.
