# Slide Count Decision: 4

- **Why not 3:** the pivot would have to share a slide with the peak confrontation ("you watched it. and said NOTHING."), muddying both the funniest beat and the warm turn. Creator's original jam phrasing was 3 slides of pure side-eye; idea room showed (and creator approved) that ending on irritation violates the page's affection rule.
- **Why not 5+:** there are only three latency crimes (not opened / opened elsewhere / seen-no-reaction collapses to two acted beats) plus one pivot. A fifth slide would either split the stare into two near-identical close-ups or pad the pivot — both kill swipe value.
- **4 is the minimum complete arc:** setup, escalation, peak, turn. Each slide has a distinct shot size and a distinct crime/answer.
