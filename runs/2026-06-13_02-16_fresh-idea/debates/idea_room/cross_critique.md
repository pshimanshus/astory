## Relatability Ethnographer Critique

`The Can We Leave Language` is a strong send because many couples have a public exit signal, but it risks becoming a generic party meme unless the visual proof is painfully specific. Eye contact alone is not enough.

`The Pocket Migration Before Leaving` has better behavior texture: the couple redistributes tiny objects before leaving. The first draft risks making Zuv only a storage unit. Repair by making the system mutual: her small emergencies go into his pocket, his charger or glasses cloth goes into her tote, and both act like this is normal.

`The Helmet Strap Tax` is deeply specific and mutual. Its only risk is becoming protective-boyfriend scooter content if the scene centers only his care. Keep both checking each other.

## Shareability Strategist Critique

`The Can We Leave Language` has the fastest tag trigger, but it is harder to make visually ownable without relying on a social crowd. The hook is excellent; the picture may be less fresh.

`The Helmet Strap Tax` is more specific and less common, but its audience is narrower. It needs a warmer hook than "helmet strap" to travel outside scooter couples.

`The Pocket Migration Before Leaving` has the widest send trigger if repaired: almost every couple has some version of "your pocket, my bag, our tiny inventory." The comment bait is natural: "his pockets are our house," "my bag is his cable drawer," "why do we do this?"

## Visual Story Director Critique

`The Can We Leave Language` can work, but parties add background noise, crowd ambiguity, and more identity-risk faces unless the scene is stripped down aggressively.

`The Helmet Strap Tax` is drawable and emotionally real, but helmets, straps, scooter, scarf, bag, and keys may compete if overpacked in one frame. It is better after story lock than as an immediate single idea pitch.

`The Pocket Migration Before Leaving` has the cleanest production path: one doorway, two readable faces, clear hands, a pocket, a tote, keys, and three tiny props maximum. It gives upper-middle negative space for text and avoids microtext.

## Room Decision

Select a repaired version of Tara's `The Pocket Migration Before Leaving`, incorporating Meera's mutual-care guardrail and Kabir's send-trigger framing.
