## Candidate Repaired

`The Pocket Migration Before Leaving`

## Problems In Raw Version

- Could collapse into a familiar "girlfriend uses boyfriend's pockets" gag.
- Too many tiny props would hurt phone readability and imagegen clarity.
- Needs a stronger emotional truth than "he carries her things."

## Repair

New title: `The Shared Pocket System`

Repaired concept:
Aachu and Zuv cannot leave the house until their tiny emergencies have migrated into the correct person. Her hair tie and lip balm go into his jacket pocket. His charger or glasses cloth slips into her tote. Neither announces it. They just redistribute the relationship inventory with the seriousness of airport security.

Payoff:
Outside, Aachu reaches her hand out without looking; Zuv instantly drops the lip balm into her palm. A beat later, Zuv reaches toward her tote and she hands him his charger. They are both ridiculous. They are both cared for.

## Why This Repair Holds

- The joke becomes mutual, not gendered.
- The visual stays one-location and prop-led.
- It has a clean send-trigger: couples who use each other's pockets, bags, chargers, tissues, hair ties, or "just keep this for me" systems.
- The emotional truth is not "he carries things"; it is "we become one tiny logistics department before leaving."
- It stays warm, not mean.

## Guardrails For Story Room

- Keep to 4 slides unless a tighter 3-slide arc proves the same escalation.
- Use no more than three tiny recurring objects: hair tie, lip balm, charger.
- Avoid object labels, phone screens, or microtext.
- Make both Aachu and Zuv actively participate.
- Keep the doorway/entryway composition airy with upper-middle negative space for baked-in handwritten text.
