# Idea Choice Shortlist

Run: `2026-06-13_02-16_fresh-idea`

Creator preference noted: future `/astory` idea locks should show multiple options before asking for approval, so the creator can choose rather than receive one crowned idea.

## Recommended

### A. The Shared Pocket System

Score: `4.81 / 5`

Hook: `we do not pack. we redistribute.`

Aachu and Zuv cannot leave until their tiny essentials silently migrate into the correct person: her hair tie and lip balm into his jacket pocket, his charger into her tote. The payoff is mutual: each produces the exact thing the other needs without asking.

Why choose this:
- Best blend of relatability, shareability, and clean visual staging.
- Mutual care, not a one-sided storage joke.
- Strong comment bait: pockets, bags, chargers, hair ties, "just keep this for me."

Risk:
- Must stay mutual or it becomes a familiar boyfriend-storage gag.

## Strong Alternatives

### B. The Can We Leave Language

Score: `4.54 / 5`

Hook: `we have a whole language for "can we leave?"`

At a social event, Aachu and Zuv use a tiny private signal to ask, answer, and confirm it is time to leave. The romance is that nobody else hears the conversation.

Why choose this:
- Fastest send-trigger.
- Very commentable: eyebrow, foot tap, keys jingle, one look.
- Captures "we are a team in public" energy.

Risk:
- Needs a very specific signal object or gesture, otherwise it becomes generic party eye contact.

### C. The Helmet Strap Tax

Score: `4.52 / 5`

Hook: `late, but still checking the helmet strap.`

Before a scooter ride, both are impatient to leave, but the scooter cannot move until they have inspected each other: helmet click, bag zip, scarf safely away from the wheel.

Why choose this:
- Most tactile and specific.
- Beautiful hidden-care behavior.
- Feels very A Story if staged as mutual fussing, not protection.

Risk:
- Slightly narrower audience; too many props can crowd the frame.

### D. Still Mad, Still Careful

Score: `4.49 / 5`

Hook: `we were still mad. he still brought my water.`

After a small fight, the care rituals continue anyway: water, charger, blanket, saved food. Nobody says sorry yet, but the body has already started repairing.

Why choose this:
- Emotionally strongest.
- High save potential.
- Good for a softer, less joke-forward carousel.

Risk:
- More familiar; needs a mutual repair gesture to avoid one-sided care.

## Recommendation

Pick A for the cleanest first carousel. Pick B if you want maximum tagging and comments. Pick C if you want the most tactile, culturally specific everyday scene. Pick D if you want tenderness over comedy.
