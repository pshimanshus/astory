## Relatability Ethnographer Critique

`The Corner Watch` is the most behavior-true: a hand appearing on a sharp table corner is a tiny, photographable act of care. It is mutual, nonverbal, and not internet-worn.

`The Booth-Side Treaty` is instantly understandable, but needs to avoid "princess entitlement" framing. The better version is that Zuv moves before Aachu even reaches the seat, because knowing her preference is the care.

`The Laundry Line Diplomacy` is tactile and specific, but it must not become a joke about one person doing chores badly. Repair by making the towel mistake harmless and the co-building affectionate.

## Shareability Strategist Critique

`The Booth-Side Treaty` has the cleanest three-second send-trigger. Couples know restaurant rituals. It will tag well.

`The Corner Watch` may be more original than viral on first glance, but it has high save/love value if the hook names the reflex clearly.

`The AC Remote Treaty` has broad shareability because temperature negotiations are common, but it risks being a familiar domestic conflict unless staged as a treaty, not a fight.

## Visual Story Director Critique

`The Laundry Line Diplomacy` has the best frame architecture: one line, repeated props, natural slide progression, airy negative space, and safe face spacing.

`The Corner Watch` is visually simple and intimate, but the repeated corner gesture needs clean staging so each slide does not feel identical.

`The Food Photo Waiting Room` is easy to understand but can drift into a familiar boyfriend-photographer / wait-for-photo meme. It needs the first-bite payoff to stay warm.

## Room Decision

Present a shortlist, not a single lock. Recommend `The Corner Watch` for emotional originality, with `The Laundry Line Diplomacy` as the strongest visual-production alternative and `The Booth-Side Treaty` as the strongest shareability alternative.
