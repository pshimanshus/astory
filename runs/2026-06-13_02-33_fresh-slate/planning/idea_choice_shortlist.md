# Fresh Idea Choice Shortlist

Run: `2026-06-13_02-33_fresh-slate`

Freshness rule: these options avoid the previous shortlist's pocket system, can-we-leave signal, helmet/scooter safety ritual, and post-fight water care.

## A. The Corner Watch

Score: `4.82 / 5`

Hook: `love is a hand on the sharp corner.`

Aachu and Zuv keep saving each other from the same table/shelf corner. One bends down, the other silently covers the edge with a palm. Later the roles reverse. Nobody says anything. The care is just automatic.

Why choose this:
- Most original emotional behavior.
- Very A Story: tiny, quiet, funny-soft care.
- Mutual, specific, and visually simple.

Risk:
- Needs smart slide variety so the repeated hand gesture does not feel same-same.

## B. The Laundry Line Diplomacy

Score: `4.71 / 5`

Hook: `we made laundry political.`

Zuv proudly hangs one giant towel across the entire clothesline. Aachu arrives with more wet clothes. What starts as a tiny domestic dispute becomes a precise, ridiculous co-built laundry gallery.

Why choose this:
- Best visual world: clothesline, pegs, towel, tiny socks.
- Fresh and tactile.
- Strong 5-slide progression.

Risk:
- Must not become "he does chores badly"; the comedy should be mutual and affectionate.

## C. The Booth-Side Treaty

Score: `4.63 / 5`

Hook: `no discussion. she gets the booth side.`

At a restaurant, Zuv automatically takes the outside chair before Aachu even asks. She notices, he pretends it was nothing, and the tiny seating ritual becomes the whole love story.

Why choose this:
- Best send/comment trigger.
- Fastest to understand.
- Very taggable for couples with date rituals.

Risk:
- Needs careful tone so it reads as knowing-care, not princess entitlement.

## D. The AC Remote Treaty

Score: `4.59 / 5`

Hook: `same room. two climates.`

Aachu is wrapped up, Zuv is warm, and the AC remote sits between them like disputed territory. They negotiate a two-climate room with a blanket, a fan, and quiet smugness.

Why choose this:
- Broadly relatable.
- Clear prop comedy.
- Very easy to stage in one room.

Risk:
- Temperature fights are familiar; the treaty staging must make it fresh.

## E. The Food Photo Waiting Room

Score: `4.55 / 5`

Hook: `he waited for the photo. she gave him first bite.`

Zuv freezes with a fork while Aachu rotates the plate for the perfect food photo. He becomes lighting assistant against his will. Payoff: after the photo, she gives him the first bite.

Why choose this:
- Funny and taggable.
- Clear visual payoff.
- Warm enough if the reward lands.

Risk:
- Can drift into a familiar food-photo meme.

## F. The Parcel Opening Committee

Score: `4.46 / 5`

Hook: `the parcel came. we kept the bubble wrap.`

They open a delivery together with too much seriousness. The actual item becomes irrelevant because the bubble wrap is the real entertainment.

Why choose this:
- Sweet, silly, low-stakes couple nonsense.
- Good safe face spacing around a box prop.
- Fresher than a quote-card romance.

Risk:
- Softer engagement than the top options; needs expressive faces and tactile bubble-wrap action.

## Recommendation

Pick A for the most A Story emotional originality. Pick B for the strongest visual carousel. Pick C for the fastest tag/share performance.
