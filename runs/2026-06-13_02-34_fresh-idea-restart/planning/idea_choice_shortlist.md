# Fresh Idea Restart Shortlist

Run: `2026-06-13_02-34_fresh-idea-restart`

This pass deliberately avoids care/logistics/usefulness and avoids copying prior winner hook shapes.

## 1. The Chairs Disagreed

**Engine:** new-love denial / body-language confession

**Hook:** `we were just friends. the chairs disagreed.`

They keep saying it is casual, but every time they sit together the chairs move closer, angle inward, and betray the truth before either of them admits it.

**Visual proof:** a cafe/home table where the chairs start apart, then slowly angle closer; a bag, knee, dupatta, or hand crosses the invisible border while both pretend nothing happened.

**Send reason:** send to the person you were "just talking" to before both of you stopped lying.

**Risk:** if staged like a generic cafe date, it dies. The furniture movement must be the joke.

## 2. Compliment Failed Successfully

**Engine:** awkward flirting / patao incompetence

**Hook:** `he had a compliment ready. his mouth chose weather.`

He rehearses something smooth. She appears. His brain exits the room and he says the least romantic sentence possible.

**Visual proof:** Zuv rehearsing with a spoon or reflection; Aachu enters; he panics into "nice weather" or an equally useless line while she catches the whole disaster.

**Send reason:** girl sends guy: "this is you." Guy sends girl: "this is why I sounded stupid."

**Risk:** needs a painfully specific failed line, not generic awkward-boy energy.

## 3. The Sock Fight Was Not About The Sock

**Engine:** attention drama / fake domestic prosecution

**Hook:** `some fights are just 'come here' written badly.`

A tiny fight starts over a sock on the floor, but the real point is that she wants him to follow her into the room and make the mood unserious again.

**Visual proof:** one sock treated like court evidence; Aachu scolding from the doorway; Zuv following with the face of someone who knows the case is fake but still must participate.

**Send reason:** send to the partner who knows your small fights are often badly disguised bids for closeness.

**Risk:** must stay mutual and tender; no "dramatic woman / patient man" flattening.

## 4. The Last Bite Court Case

**Engine:** petty married war / food evidence

**Hook:** `no one ate the last bite. it simply disappeared with witnesses.`

The last bite is gone. Both are innocent. Both have evidence. The dinner table briefly becomes a courtroom.

**Visual proof:** empty plate, two serious faces, one fork held like evidence, both overcommitted to innocence.

**Send reason:** send to the partner who says "I only took a little."

**Risk:** high comment potential but less premium; needs a soft final beat to avoid pure meme.

## Room Recommendation

Test `The Chairs Disagreed` first. It feels least like the old batch and has the cleanest visual metaphor. If that does not land, test `Compliment Failed Successfully` because it has the clearest send-confession.
