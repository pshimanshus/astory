# Idea Room Cross-Critique

Run: `2026-06-13_02-34_fresh-shareable-ideas`

## Relatability Ethnographer Critique

- `The Real Date Starts After We Leave` is highly sendable, but it is a ritual people already recognize. It needs a sharper visual proof than "talking after a party" or it risks becoming caption-led.
- `The Charger Has A Favorite Person` is clean and stageable, but the emotional truth is lighter than the prop. It may read as a cute tech inconvenience unless the payoff proves they wanted the same corner, not just the same cable.
- `The Packet Pause` is the freshest behavior: not grand care, not generic support, just ridiculous self-control for the person you love. The packet itself proves the joke.

## Shareability Strategist Critique

- `The Door Was Never Fully Closed` has beautiful emotional voltage, but it may generate saves more than sends unless the fight stays petty and funny. It also sits near the prior "Still Mad, Still Careful" territory.
- `The Packet Pause` has a strong tag reason: send it to the person who eats like the whole house has joined the call, or the person who freezes to protect your call. The comment bait is immediate and warm.
- `The Charger Has A Favorite Person` is visually strong but less universal as a share trigger; some couples will see it, but fewer will feel personally accused.

## Visual Story Director Critique

- `The Packet Pause` stages cleanly: one laptop, one snack packet, one frozen body, one guilty face. It has clear negative space for text and no need for readable screen UI.
- `The Real Date Starts After We Leave` needs a vehicle, doorway, or walk-home scene to become visual; otherwise it becomes two people talking under a clever line.
- `The Door Was Never Fully Closed` is drawable, but the tone is delicate. Push too sad and it becomes breakup-adjacent; push too comic and it loses the tenderness.

## Room Decision

`The Packet Pause` survives best after critique because it is:

- fresh against the prior run's shortlist,
- specific enough to avoid quote-card romance,
- visually provable in one glance,
- funny without making either partner a fool,
- flexible enough for a mutual repair in story room.

The room repairs it from a one-person gag into a mutual "household sound choreography" idea before selection.
