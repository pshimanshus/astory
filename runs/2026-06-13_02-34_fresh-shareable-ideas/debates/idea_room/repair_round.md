## Repaired Winner

### The Packet Pause

Original risk:

- The first version could read as "Aachu is noisy while Zuv is serious."
- It needed mutuality so the joke feels like couple choreography, not one partner being the problem.

Repair:

- Make the behavior a shared household protocol: whoever is off-call freezes the dangerous object.
- Aachu freezes with a half-open snack packet during Zuv's call.
- Later, Zuv freezes mid-spoon-clink or mid-cup-place while Aachu is recording a voice note or on her own call.
- The payoff is not "one supports the other"; it is "we both know which household sounds are illegal during your call."

Why the repair works:

- It keeps the absurd, photographable behavior.
- It removes one-sided stereotype risk.
- It creates a stronger carousel arc than a single gag.
- It gives story room a natural 4-slide escalation without needing screen UI or microtext.

Draft hook options:

- `love is knowing which sounds are illegal during a call.`
- `do not open that packet. i am on a call.`
- `we have call etiquette. it is mostly snack fear.`
- `supportive partner? no. silent packet specialist.`
