# Idea Choice Shortlist

Run: `2026-06-13_02-34_fresh-shareable-ideas`

## Recommended

### A. The Packet Pause

Score: `4.82 / 5`

Hook: `love is knowing which sounds are illegal during a call.`

During each other's calls or voice notes, Aachu and Zuv freeze mid-snack-opening, mid-spoon-clink, or mid-cup-place because certain household sounds are apparently illegal now.

Why choose this:

- Freshest idea in the room.
- Very shareable: every couple has a loud object that becomes suspiciously louder during calls.
- Easy to stage with one room, one laptop, one packet, one cup/spoon, and readable faces.
- The repaired version is mutual, so it does not turn one partner into the joke.

Risk:

- Needs the role reversal beat. Without it, the idea becomes "one partner is noisy."

## Strong Alternatives

### B. The Door Was Never Fully Closed

Score: `4.71 / 5`

Hook: `mad, but not door-closed mad.`

After a small fight, one of them goes to the other room but leaves the door slightly open, because the argument needs distance but love does not.

Why choose this:

- Best emotional voltage.
- One object carries the entire feeling.
- Very A Story if kept petty, domestic, and tender.

Risk:

- Close to the earlier "Still Mad, Still Careful" lane and easy to over-sadden.

### C. The Real Date Starts After We Leave

Score: `4.66 / 5`

Hook: `the real date starts after we leave.`

Aachu and Zuv leave a social thing and immediately begin the private couple debrief: who was sweet, who was weird, what they both noticed but could not say out loud.

Why choose this:

- Cleanest send hook.
- Huge comment bait for post-party debrief couples.
- Could become a strong 4-slide social-to-private arc.

Risk:

- Needs a very specific car, scooter, doorway, or walk-home proof or it becomes caption-led.

### D. The Charger Has A Favorite Person

Score: `4.62 / 5`

Hook: `the charger has a favorite person.`

One long charging cable keeps migrating toward whoever is pretending they do not need attention, until both phones are abandoned beside one outlet and the couple is sitting together anyway.

Why choose this:

- Best visual prop.
- Low production risk.
- Good for a soft, tactile, domestic carousel.

Risk:

- Slightly lighter emotional truth; can read as a tech inconvenience unless the proximity payoff is strong.

### E. The Subtitles Came On Without Discussion

Score: `4.49 / 5`

Hook: `i turned subtitles on for your chips.`

One person is eating something too crunchy during a movie, so the other silently turns subtitles on instead of starting a fight.

Why choose this:

- Funny, simple, and very recognizable.
- Good warm comment bait.

Risk:

- More familiar; must avoid sitcom-generic staging.

## Recommendation

Pick A if you want the freshest, most photographable share trigger. Pick B if you want emotional tenderness. Pick C if you want maximum tagging. Pick D if you want the cleanest visual prop.
