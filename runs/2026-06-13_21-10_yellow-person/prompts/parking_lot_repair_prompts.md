# Parking-Lot Confession Repair Prompts

## Repair Contract

Use the "yellow person" screenshots only for concept/text. Use best-illustration references only as a craft quality bar, not as a scene, wardrobe, pose, or composition template.

Hard avoids:
- no lake or waterfront hug
- no bedroom/floor vulnerability scene
- no white oversized shirt + navy tee pairing
- no copied observational-intimacy centered domestic layout
- no generic tender couple in room
- no blue wash as the whole visual idea
- no yellow/parchment/sepia/beige/tan/heavy-cream page cast

Fresh direction:
Parked car after rain, late night. Aachu and Zuv share a vulnerable conversation in privacy. The "yellow" is proven by a small local dashboard/streetlight warmth on hands/faces, not by a yellow page or copied reference palette.

## Slide 1 Text

Who is your yellow?

## Slide 1 Scene

Inside a parked car after rain, seen from a slightly elevated passenger-side/front angle. Rain beads on the window, blurred streetlights outside, quiet black/charcoal car interior. Aachu sits in the passenger seat turned slightly toward the window, emotionally softened after saying something vulnerable. Zuv sits beside her, not performing romance, simply holding her hand over the center console with steady attention. Wardrobe must be fresh: Aachu in muted sage or dusty mauve knit/top, Zuv in charcoal overshirt or muted rust/brown tee. No white oversized shirt, no navy tee.

## Slide 2 Text

A Yellow Person:
Someone you can tell absolutely
everything to and they won't judge you.
Someone you can trust with all your heart.

## Slide 2 Scene

Same parked car, closer and more grounded. Aachu is mid-confession with one hand near her chest or resting on a small folded receipt/tissue/journal in her lap; Zuv is angled toward her, listening fully, one hand gently covering hers on the center console. The windshield/roof area leaves upper negative space for text. Keep the scene modern, private, and specific; no bedroom, no floor sitting, no previous observational-intimacy wardrobe or room language.
