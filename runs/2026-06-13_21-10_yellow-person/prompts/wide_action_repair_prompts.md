# Wide Action Repair Prompts

## Active Repair Rules

Rejected patterns:
- concept screenshot style, yellow cast, quote-card texture
- observational-intimacy wardrobe, bedroom/floor/domestic template, centered copied layout
- tight two-head close-ups
- both faces turned sideways/away
- cheek-on-shoulder, face-nestled, generic quiet-couple closeness

New visual direction:
Wide, action-led stairwell/landing sequence. The "yellow person" is proven by Zuv making a nonjudgmental pocket of safety for Aachu: showing up, giving her space, setting down ordinary comfort objects, and listening without crowding her.

## Slide 1

Text:
Who is your yellow?

Scene:
Apartment stairwell landing after rain, night. Aachu sits on the landing step in a muted plum sweatshirt and dark pants, front/three-quarter face readable, holding a crumpled tissue or folded note. Zuv is a few steps below or near the open doorway, facing three-quarter toward her with his face readable, carrying two paper cups and a small convenience-store bag. He is not crowding her; the emotional proof is the space he gives her. A small local hallway/elevator glow touches the cups and railing only.

## Slide 2

Text:
A Yellow Person:
Someone you can tell absolutely
everything to and they won't judge you.
Someone you can trust with all your heart.

Scene:
Same stairwell landing, wider and calmer. Aachu is seated front/three-quarter on the step with the note/journal open in her lap, face readable. Zuv sits one step lower but offset, not shoulder-to-shoulder, facing her three-quarter/front with steady attention. Between them: two paper cups, a small snack packet, and his phone placed face-down on the step. The image should read "he is listening and not judging" even if the text is covered.
