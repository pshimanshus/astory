## Meera On Kabir: `The Little Instructions He Learned`

Strong send engine, but "care instructions" can become soft meme-language unless
the carousel names one embarrassingly specific rule only this couple would know.
The behavior is present: water, charger, blanket, and open doorway. The first
slide still risks leaning on Aachu alone with a phone draft, which needs text to
carry too much.

Repair from Meera: show the instruction before naming it. Example: he leaves
the door open exactly enough, places water where the door will not hit it, and
does not ask "what happened?" until she touches the glass.

Main risk: Zuv becomes the competent handler and Aachu becomes the difficult
object with settings. Repairable, but only if the story keeps mutual humanity.

## Meera On Tara: `The Chair Outside The Door`

This is photographable without caption: closed door, chair, water, charger,
folded cloth, light under the door. The love is behavior, not sentiment.

Meera's hard concern: Tara's original "Zuv asleep in the chair" payoff tips
toward martyr staging. The stronger payoff is ordinary: Aachu cracks the door
and uses what he left. No sleeping saint, no hug, no speech.

Vote: `The Chair Outside The Door`.

## Kabir On The Room

Kabir's dissent: `The Little Instructions He Learned` is the cleaner Instagram
send. It has the most taggable hook and comment bait: everyone has a care rule.
`The Chair Outside The Door` is the better short film, but its send line needs
sharpening.

Kabir repair for the selected route: keep the chair, but make the send trigger
explicit: "thank you for not making me explain immediately." The hook should be
behavior-specific, not only poetic.

Vote: `The Little Instructions He Learned`.

## Tara On The Room

Tara votes for `The Chair Outside The Door` as the cleanest production idea.
Door, chair, water, charger, folded cloth: all readable visual nouns. It does
not need both faces, avoids microtext, and has low identity-drift risk.

Tara's hard condition: five slides of a door become dead unless object logic
changes. The chair must move, the glass must empty, the charger must cross the
threshold, and the light must change. No final hug. No martyr Zuv asleep like a
saint. The payoff is: he knew when not to knock.

Vote: `The Chair Outside The Door`.

## Shared Rejections

- Sad close-up plus a pasted "am I hard to love?" question.
- A perfect reassurance speech as the answer.
- Both Aachu and Zuv forced into every slide.
- Patient-boyfriend-fixes-anxious-girlfriend framing.
- Concept screenshot color/style copied into final A Story visuals.
- Phone microtext, label text, dense map UI, or unreadable care notes.

## Room Decision

Select `The Chair Outside The Door`, repaired with Kabir's "learned care rules"
engine:

- Hook lane: `am i hard to love when i go quiet?`
- Behavioral proof: do not knock again; leave water, charger, cloth, and enough
  closeness to be heard.
- Payoff: Aachu uses what he left. Zuv is not framed as a martyr.
- Carry forward: chair distance, glass level, charger path, door crack, and
  softened light must visibly change across slides.
