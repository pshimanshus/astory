## Selected Candidate

`The Chair Outside The Door`

## Problem Found In Cross-Critique

The raw chair idea is emotionally and visually strongest, but it has two risks:

- It can become too heavy to send if the hook stays only poetic.
- It can become saint/martyr staging if Zuv is shown suffering in the chair.

Kabir's `The Little Instructions He Learned` had the stronger send engine, but
its phrasing risked meme-template care-label energy and visual phone/draft
dependence.

## Repair Applied

Fuse the two:

- Keep the chair/door object spine.
- Import the "learned care rule" behavior, not the care-instruction wording.
- First-slide hook direction: `am i hard to love when i go quiet?`
- The answer is never a speech. It is object continuity:
  - chair close enough to stay, far enough not to pressure
  - water placed where the door will not hit it
  - charger slid within reach
  - folded cloth left on the chair back
  - light under the door changing with time
- Payoff: Aachu cracks the door and uses what he left.
- Hard ban: no sleeping martyr, no dramatic embrace, no "you were never hard to
  love" quote-card finish.

## Constraints Carried Forward

- Some slides should show only Aachu, only objects, or only partial Zuv
  presence.
- The chair is practical, not sacrificial.
- Keep the tone intimate and ordinary.
- Make the objects change across the carousel so five slides do not become the
  same door repeated.
- Exact story and slide count remain unlocked until the creator approves the
  idea.
