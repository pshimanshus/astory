# Idea Room Cross-Critique

Run: `2026-06-14_02-21_hard-to-love`

## Meera On The Room

The concept dies if it stays at "am I hard to love?" as a sad sentence. The strongest candidates translate insecurity into behavior: waiting outside a door, packing a care kit, lowering a lamp, saving a bite, keeping an exit open. "The Chair Outside The Door" is strongest because the love is restraint. It does not make Aachu perform distress or Zuv perform heroism.

## Kabir On The Room

"The Chair Outside The Door" needs a sharper first-line hook to become a send, not just a beautiful moment. The repair is to fuse the question with the behavior: `am i hard to love when i go quiet?` That gives someone a reason to send it to the partner who waits, gives space, or loves in actions more than words.

## Tara On The Room

"The Chair Outside The Door" is the cleanest visual carrier. The chair, door, water, charger, folded cloth, and morning light can tell the story without forcing both people into every frame. It avoids the stale sad portrait, the crowded couple close-up, and the dark foreign style from the concept screenshots.

## Shared Rejections

- Do not make this a quote-card sequence of sad questions.
- Do not force Aachu and Zuv into every slide.
- Do not turn Zuv into a saintly rescuer or Aachu into a problem to solve.
- Do not copy the magenta/orange/dark concept style; the approved style is the airy best-illustration watercolor-and-ink look.
- Do not depend on phone microtext, label text, or complex crowd detail.
