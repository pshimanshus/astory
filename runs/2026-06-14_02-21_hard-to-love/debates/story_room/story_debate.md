## Room Consensus

The storyboard should be five slides. Dev and Nina both land on the same structure: hook, first practical care action, learned distance, accepted object, quiet payoff.

The strongest version is not the most poetic version. The text should become more observed as the carousel progresses:

1. `am i hard to love when i go quiet?`
2. `or did you learn where to leave the water?`
3. `you learned the chair distance`
4. `the words came later. the charger came first.`
5. `i was not hard to love. i was hard to rush.`

## What Changed In The Merge

- Slide 2 uses Nina's more concrete water line instead of a more abstract "near, not knocking" line.
- Slide 3 keeps Dev's chair-distance spine and uses Arjun's retention fix: `you learned the chair distance`.
- Slide 4 keeps Nina's charger-first acceptance line because it gives the carousel its most observed behavior.
- Slide 5 keeps the direct answer to the hook, but only after the objects have already earned it.

## Rejected Beats

- Zuv asleep or waiting all night in the chair.
- A separate crying-alone Aachu slide.
- A slide for every care object.
- A speech-bubble explanation.
- A final hug, apology, or face-to-face reconciliation.

## Story-Lock Risks

- Static-door repetition: each slide needs a different camera or threshold state.
- Over-subtle continuity: water level, charger path, chair distance, and door crack must read at phone size.
- Identity evaporation: later slides should give Aachu at least a partial, owned presence without forcing both faces.
- Saintly-Zuv drift: Zuv's care must stay practical, not performative.
- Prompt drift: prompt room must not override the creator's no-forced-both constraint.

## Recommendation

Lock the five-slide storyboard and selected scenes, then proceed to character/style bible and prompt pack only after creator approval.
