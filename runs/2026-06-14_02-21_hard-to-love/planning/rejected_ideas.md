# Rejected Ideas

Run: `2026-06-14_02-21_hard-to-love`

## Strong But Not Selected

### The Little Instructions He Learned

Best send/comment engine. It lost because the wording risks care-label meme
energy and can frame Aachu as a difficult object with settings. Its strongest
piece was preserved inside the selected idea: learned care as behavior.

### The Bag That Knew Already

Good taggable hook and strong prop engine, but it risks becoming a care-kit
list or Pinterest romance. Strong backup if the creator wants a lighter, more
playful lane.

### The Lamp Left Low

Emotionally close to the request, but its visual engine is softer and could
become atmosphere without enough concrete proof. Use only if the creator wants a
bedroom/room-softening story.

## Rejected For This Run

### The Best Bite Under The Bowl

Beautiful and safe, but it shifts the concept from "am I hard to love?" into
"am I enough because I give?" That risks making Aachu prove worth through
selflessness.

### The Exit Already Open

Strong metaphor, but more fragile for imagegen: crowd, phone route, door,
stairs, and body angle all have to land. It also has a higher savior-staging
risk.
