# Slide Count Decision

Run: `2026-06-14_02-21_hard-to-love`

Recommended slide count: **5**

## Why Five

This story cannot land as a single "hard to love" quote over a sad doorway. The proof is not the line; the proof is the chair changing distance, the water being placed safely, the charger crossing the threshold, and Aachu accepting care before she has words.

Five slides give the viewer one new piece of knowledge each time:

1. The fear: quiet might make her hard to love.
2. The first answer: he leaves care without knocking.
3. The correction: he learns distance, not pressure.
4. The acceptance: she reaches for care before words.
5. The payoff: she was not hard to love, just hard to rush.

## Why Fewer Fails

Three slides would become hook, gesture, payoff. That skips time and calibration, so Zuv looks magically perfect and the chair becomes a symbol instead of behavior.

Four slides would force a merge: either time-passing gets folded into acceptance, or acceptance gets folded into payoff. Both merges weaken the object proof.

## Why More Dilutes

Six or more slides would turn water, charger, cloth, light, and chair distance into prop accounting. Extra waiting slides also risk making Zuv look saintly, which the approved idea explicitly rejects.

## Cut Or Keep

| Beat | Verdict | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Closed door and practical chair hook | keep | The recognition slide and emotional contract. |
| Water and charger placed outside door swing | keep | Active care without pressure. |
| Chair moved back, light and objects changed | keep | Turns one nice act into learned care. |
| Door crack and charger accepted | keep | Aachu regains agency before explanation. |
| Changed-object payoff | keep | Answers the hook without hug or speech. |
| Zuv asleep or waiting all night in the chair | cut | Martyr staging. |
| Separate slide for each care object | cut | Prop inventory, not story. |
| Dramatic embrace or apology finale | cut | Generic romance closure. |
| Aachu crying alone behind the door | cut | Sad quote-card gravity and weak agency. |

## Story-Lock Recommendation

Proceed with five slides, but only if the visual treatment keeps every frame changing: door distance, object state, threshold openness, and camera angle must all move.
