# Idea Room Cross-Critique

Run: `2026-06-14_17-51_ig-reference-concept`

## Shared Premise

The reference mechanic is not the quote-card. It is the tiny exchange: someone is surprised a detail was remembered, and the other person treats remembering as obvious.

Rejected generic version:

> Love is when they remember the little things.

That is the dead version. The A Story version needs an object, timing, and a tiny private habit.

## Agent Critiques

### Meera On Kabir

Kabir's strongest idea is `The Hair Clip In His Pocket`. It passes the behavior test because it is not an abstract preference; it is a repeated panic moment. The risk is making Zuv too saintly or Aachu too helpless. Repair: he does not fix her hair. He only produces the exact old clip and lets her handle it.

`He Ordered The Backup Fries` has high shareability but is more internet-worn. It can work only if the food refusal is deeply Aachu-specific, otherwise it becomes the old girlfriend-steals-food joke.

### Kabir On Meera

Meera's `The Phone Was Already Charging` is very sendable, but it is slightly quieter as a first-frame hook. It becomes strong if the cable geography is obvious in frame, weak if the viewer has to read a phone screen.

`The AC Number He Pretends Is Random` has comment bait but overlaps recent AC memory. Keep it as a backup, not the lead, unless the creator wants continuity.

### Tara On Both

The hair clip wins visually because it can be read without a caption: hair in face, hand in pocket, exact clip, Aachu's caught expression. It avoids the stale close-angle two-face composition and does not require microtext.

The charger idea is the safest imagegen candidate but risks a flatter emotional payoff. The food-order idea is commentable but could need receipt/order microtext, which is a production risk.

## Room Decision

All three agents independently selected the hair-clip direction. It is the best match for:

- reference mechanic: remembered tiny detail;
- local winner pattern: specific emotional behavior over quote-card wisdom;
- platform mechanic: send/comment trigger;
- A Story production: object-led scene with clear visual proof.

The concept is safe to present for `HITL_IDEA_LOCK` only after the repaired scene-landing preview is written.
