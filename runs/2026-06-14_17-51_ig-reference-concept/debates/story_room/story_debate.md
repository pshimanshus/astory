# Story Room Debate

Run: `2026-06-14_17-51_ig-reference-concept`

Selected idea: `The Hair Clip He Started Carrying`

Reference mechanic extracted: intimacy as proof of remembered micro-behavior. The carousel should not copy the reference card format, quote structure, or pink visual style. The object has to do the emotional work.

## Evidence Basis

- Local outcome evidence favored carousels with specific, sendable couple behavior over abstract love lessons.
- The run's reference analysis identified the useful mechanic as: "you remembered a tiny thing I never formally asked you to remember."
- The anti-slop gate rejected moral lines like `real love is the little things`, `you remembered?`, and `of course I did` because they would drift into quote-card romance.

## Agent Inputs

### Story Director

Recommended a 5-slide arc built around the clip appearing from Zuv's pocket, with the payoff staying small and physically grounded. Key warning: do not show Zuv fixing Aachu's hair, and do not turn the moment into a spoken confession.

### Pacing Editor

Recommended a 4-slide version to avoid over-explaining surprise. Strongest note: each slide must add a new piece of information, not repeat Aachu being shocked.

### Swipe Retention Critic

Recommended a 5-slide final sequence:

1. `why do you have my clip?`
2. `wait. my old one?`
3. `it lives in my pocket now.`
4. `for first-bite emergencies.`
5. `fine. keep it there.`

Key warning: ending with `you remember everything` or `little things matter` kills the object-led charm.

## Coordinator Decision

Lock the draft as 5 slides.

The 4-slide version is clean, but it compresses two separate pleasures: the reveal that the clip has a home in his pocket, and the reason it lives there. The 5-slide version gives every swipe a fresh reveal:

1. Object mystery.
2. Personal object proof.
3. Habit reveal.
4. Timing joke.
5. Acceptance payoff.

## Slop Rejections

- Rejected: `you remembered? / of course I did.` Reason: too close to reference phrasing and too quote-card.
- Rejected: `real love is when he remembers the little things.` Reason: generic relationship page language.
- Rejected: Zuv clipping Aachu's hair for her. Reason: shifts the beat into caretaker romance instead of known-chaos romance.
- Rejected: a cozy table scene with no physical proof. Reason: visual mood without object logic.

## Story Lock Risk Notes

- The clip must visibly be Aachu's old clip, not a random pretty accessory.
- Zuv should not perform sweetness. He should be casual, almost annoyingly prepared.
- Aachu should clip her own hair in the final frame.
- Food timing matters. Without the first-bite setup, the clip is just a cute prop.
