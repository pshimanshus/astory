# Slide Count Decision

Run: `2026-06-14_17-51_ig-reference-concept`

Selected count: **5 slides**

## Why 5 Wins

The story needs five distinct reveals:

1. Aachu catches Zuv with her clip.
2. She realizes it is her old one.
3. He reveals it now lives in his pocket.
4. The reason is her first-bite hair chaos.
5. She approves the system while pretending not to.

## Why Fewer Fails

A 4-slide version can work, but it has to merge either the pocket reveal or the food-timing explanation. That makes the remembered-detail mechanic feel more efficient than funny. The viewer loses one swipe of "wait, he does this regularly?"

## Why More Dilutes

Six or more slides would overplay Aachu's surprise or add a sentimental explanation the object does not need. The charm is that no one gives a speech.

## Locked Sequence

1. `why do you have my clip?`
2. `wait. my old one?`
3. `it lives in my pocket now.`
4. `for first-bite emergencies.`
5. `fine. keep it there.`
