# Scene Landing Preview

## Recommended First Frame

Exact first-slide text / exact hook text:

```text
he said "i'm not hungry"
so why is my plate nervous?
```

First-frame visual: Aachu sits cross-legged on a living-room rug or low sofa edge with a plate of fries/snacks held close. Zuv sits slightly behind and to the side, phone loose in one hand, eyes drifting toward the food while pretending innocence. The plate is the emotional object. His hand has not stolen yet; the suspense is in the eyeline.

## Swipe Reason

Why the first swipe happens: the viewer wants the second slide because the first frame sets up the contradiction: he claims no hunger, but the plate is already in danger.

## Mini Slide Arc

1. He says he is not hungry; the plate already looks unsafe.
2. His "taste check" takes the best bite.
3. Aachu starts guarding the plate.
4. He gets offended when she moves it.
5. Payoff: he did not want food; he wanted her food.

## Payoff Frame

She gives him the last bite while acting annoyed, and he smiles like this was always the plan.

## Share Trigger

Why someone sends it: send-to-partner accusation: "this is you." The joke is affectionate enough to send without starting a fight.

## Flat/Generic Risk

What could make it land flat/generic: the image becomes a generic couple eating together.

Correction if it feels flat: make the plate visibly protected by Aachu, make Zuv's eyeline betray him, keep his phone as a fake-distraction prop, and avoid romantic leaning/pose cliches.
