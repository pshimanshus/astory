# Repair Round

## Selected Repair

Original visual seed: `she said 'main ja rahi hoon'... barefoot.`

Risk: The first version can make Aachu look childish and Zuv look saintly.

Repair:

- Aachu is not foolish; she is angry enough to leave before remembering her chappals.
- Zuv does not chase, block, lecture, or smile smugly.
- The love is in the logistics: he turns the chappals toward the door, slides water near the threshold, and leaves space for her pride.
- She returns "only for the chappals," not because she is defeated.
- The final slide keeps the fight unresolved but the distance smaller.

## Line Repair

Working first slide:

`she said 'main ja rahi hoon'... barefoot.`

Alternate if we want more Hinglish:

`main ja rahi hoon, she said. chappal andar hi reh gayi.`

Decision: use the first line. It is shorter, cleaner, and visually immediate.
