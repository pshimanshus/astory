# Rejected Ideas

## Quote-Card Adaptations

Rejected: any direct rewrite of external winner posts that only changes words.

Reason: weak wrapper, copyright-evasion risk, and not A Story-specific.

## Generic Ordinary Love

Rejected: "love is in the small things" carousels.

Reason: the bank already shows ordinary love works, but A Story needs the exact object: chappals, dhaniya, bent spoon, blurry lunch, covered roti.

## Perfect Patient Boy / Dramatic Girl

Rejected: Aachu as chaos and Zuv as saint.

Reason: the owned winner works because emotional intensity is held with tenderness, not because one person is a problem to be managed.

## Phone Screenshot Carousel

Rejected: tiny fake chat bubbles as the whole post.

Reason: message-native energy is useful, but final illustration must show the body, room, and object around the phone.
