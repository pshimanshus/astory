# Said-Feeling Rewrite

Status: replacement for `winner_bank_style_rewrite.md`.

Correction applied: the copy must feel spoken, not assembled. Every slide below has to pass one test: could someone actually say this to their person in simple words?

Boundary: this is not synonym-swapped copyright evasion. The source-bank forms are used as evidence for pacing and emotional mechanics; the lines below are original A Story-native copy.

## House Rule For This Pass

- Write like a message first, carousel second.
- No filler slide just to complete a list.
- No line that sounds like a lesson.
- No object-led joke unless the feeling already lands without it.
- The visual wrapper supports the line; it does not become the idea.
- Say the unsaid love thing plainly.
- Keep sentences short and easy.
- No pretentious love-copy voice.

## What Changed From The Rejected Pass

- Every line is written as a sentence someone could send, not a content pillar.
- The source-bank forms stay visible, but they are not treated as templates to fill.
- Visual wrappers are restrained; they prove the feeling without becoming quirky behavior.
- No chappal, dhaniya, roti, spoon, or object-led invention leads the premise.
- The copy aims for "I needed to say this" before "this is a carousel."
- Lines are now shorter, simpler, and less abstract.
- The goal is the unsaid love thing, said plainly.
