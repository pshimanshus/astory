# Winner-Bank Style Rewrite

Status: superseded by `said_feeling_rewrite.md`.

Do not use this file as the current copy source. It preserves some winner-bank hook phrasing too closely, including the daily-life concept later corrected in `creator_direction_notes.md`.

Correction applied: the previous pack over-invented behaviors. This version follows the winner-bank style more closely: text-forward slides, direct emotional premise, simple list cadence, minimal plot, and A Story visual wrapper only as support.

Boundary: no external copyright-evasion synonym swaps. Where a source is permissioned/owned, preserve the engine closely. Where not, keep the format mechanics and write distinct A Story copy.

## Bank Forms To Use

- Direct send line: `send this to them`
- Definition carousel: `when I say ___, I mean:`
- Intimacy question: `so what is real intimacy?`
- Chapter/title prompt: `what would you title this chapter?`
- Memory list: `things that used to feel normal but now feel like memories`
- First-person affirmation: `I am... / I am learning... / I am allowed...`
- Minimal caption, because the slide copy carries the feeling.

## 1. Not A Calm Girl

Source fit:

- Primary: owned `DYJpjt9CQYY` emotional-chaos/patient-love engine.
- Bank form: direct-send carousel + first-person list.

Why this replaces the chappal version:

The chappal scene was too much invented behavior. The winning style is the emotional thesis itself, carried slide by slide.

Slide count: 7

### Slides

1. `send this to the person who did not get the calm version of you.`
   Visual wrapper: Aachu and Zuv in a simple domestic frame, not doing much; the text is the hero.

2. `the person who met the overthinking, over-feeling, "mujhe kuch nahi hua" version.`
   Visual wrapper: Aachu turned away slightly; Zuv nearby, not forcing eye contact.

3. `the version that gets quiet first and explains badly later.`
   Visual wrapper: two cups on a table, one untouched.

4. `the version that says "leave me alone" but still hopes you stay close.`
   Visual wrapper: distance on sofa or bed edge, not a dramatic exit.

5. `the version that cries after sounding angry.`
   Visual wrapper: Aachu looking down; Zuv keeping physical space.

6. `thank you for not making me feel too much for being human.`
   Visual wrapper: Zuv passing water/tissue without making it a scene.

7. `maybe love is not finding someone calm. maybe it is feeling safe when you are not.`
   Visual wrapper: both in the same frame, quiet, unresolved but softer.

Caption:

send this to the person who learned your storm language without calling it drama.

Comment prompt:

Who got the not-calm version of you?

Do not do:

- Chappal gag.
- Comedy-first staging.
- Therapy explanation.

## 2. Sorry For The Tone

Source fit:

- Primary: repair/staying mechanic from winner bank and owned `DZNl2zxiRD4` mutual-effort engine.
- Bank form: short direct statement carousel.

Why this works:

This is closest to the bank style: one line people send because they cannot type it themselves.

Slide count: 5

### Slides

1. `sorry for the tone.`
   Visual wrapper: simple text-led frame; Aachu with phone or handwritten note.

2. `not sorry for needing to be heard.`
   Visual wrapper: Zuv seated across from her, listening.

3. `sometimes the feeling comes out wrong because it sat inside too long.`
   Visual wrapper: quiet room, no big action.

4. `thank you for listening past the bad delivery.`
   Visual wrapper: two cups/quiet eye contact; no lecture.

5. `we can repair the way it was said without pretending it did not hurt.`
   Visual wrapper: both in same frame, smaller distance.

Caption:

send this when you are ready to soften, but not ready to lie about why it hurt.

Comment prompt:

Apology accepted, conversation reopened?

Do not do:

- Courtroom joke.
- "Point was correct" smugness as final tone.
- Clean relationship advice.
