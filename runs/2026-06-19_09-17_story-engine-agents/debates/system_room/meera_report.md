## Evidence Basis

Read: `creative_brief.json`, novelty doc, permissioned winner workflow,
outcome attribution, run lessons, current `source_winner_novelty_model.json`,

Stale obvious version: "add storytelling techniques to the system: hooks,

Human version: force every storytelling mechanic to point back to one source
winner, one send/save behavior, and one visible couple receipt.

## Human-Truth Requirements

A `winner_story_engine` artifact must capture:

- exact source winner and what must stay: copy, premise, caption, slide
  structure, not just "the mechanic";
- share/save reason in human terms: who sends this, to whom, and what they are
  trying to say without saying it;
- stale obvious version being rejected;
- lived behavior receipt: object, gesture, silence, timing, body position,
  repeated line, or domestic action;
- photographable scene test: could the truth be understood before the caption
  explains it?
- mutual dignity test: would the partner being teased still send it?
- `but/therefore` chain translated into relationship behavior, not plot
  gymnastics;
- final payoff as a private recognizer, not a moral;
- exact lines/frames the system must not polish into therapy-page language.

## Generic Behavior Rejected

Reject any system that says: "old topic + new reveal + contrast + urgency =
novelty."

That becomes fake intelligence fast. It produces clean labels like "quiet care
after conflict" and then invents a cup, pillow, door, or charger to prove it.
That is the machine decorating an abstract lesson.

Also reject:

- mechanics-only winner abstraction;
- hook checklists outranking source causality;
- "Aachu/Zuv wrapper" meaning faces beside quote-card text;
- object-led invention that replaces the source winner;
- every beat joined by "and then";
- polished lines that sound correct but unsendable.

## Over-Systematization Risks

- "New reveal" becomes a clever sentence, not a lived observation.
- Source winners get sanitized until the thing that worked is gone.
- Aachu and Zuv become demonstration props for a concept.
- The system invents quirky domestic objects because it thinks specificity
  means props.
- Conflict loops become melodrama, when sometimes the real `therefore` is
  quieter: someone is still angry, therefore they serve the plate anyway.
- We chase curiosity instead of sendability.

## Recommended Slice

Extend the current `source_winner_novelty_model.json` into a stricter
`winner_story_engine` artifact and add QA blocks:

- `BEHAVIOR_RECEIPT_MISSING`
- `SOURCE_ENGINE_DRIFT`
- `SHARE_TRIGGER_MISSING`
- `GENERIC_NOVELTY_LABEL`
- `ONE_SIDED_JOKE`
- `EXPLAINS_THE_LESSON`

Do this before building a rich matcher. First make it impossible for an
abstract novelty model to pass as human truth.
