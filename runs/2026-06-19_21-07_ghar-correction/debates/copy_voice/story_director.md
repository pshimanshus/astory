## Story problem

Slide 1 already says the big metaphor:

`Khaali hai jo tere bina, main woh ghar hoon tera.`

So Slide 2 cannot define home, repeat "you feel like home", or explain why the lyric is deep. The story move has to be smaller: first the speaker is an empty house, then one tiny sign of return makes him open again.

What changes between slides:
- Slide 1: I am khaali without you.
- Slide 2: I do not need a new place. I need your return to unlock me.

The payoff hides in the moment of arrival, not in a moral. Key, door, cup, switch, or sound can work, but only one should carry the feeling.

## What to cut

- "Log samajhte hain..." because it starts a lecture.
- "Tere bina main bas ek jagah hoon" because it is a clean thesis, not a confession.
- "Home is a person" because that is the stale surface.
- Room-light-day lists unless the list becomes a real proof.
- Any final line that sounds like it wants applause for being deep.

## Top pick

Candidate 1.

## Why the payoff lands

The key does not decorate the emotion. It changes the speaker's state. "Band" to "khul gaya" is the actual story spine: empty house to returned-home. It also keeps the Ghar lyric alive without re-explaining it. The viewer finishes the missing word themselves: if your key opens me, I am not just a place. I am your ghar.

Candidate 1 is also the least written. It has one confession, one proof, one turn. No definition. No clean moral. No "real love is..." smell.
