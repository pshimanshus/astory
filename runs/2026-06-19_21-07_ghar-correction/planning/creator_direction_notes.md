# Creator Direction Notes

## 2026-06-19 - Ghar copy repair

Creator rejected the first two-slide copy for the "Ghar" song-line concept as
cliche. The rejected assumption was that a direct emotional note about "you feel
like home" was enough.

Active constraint: for direct A Story carousel copy, especially when the creator
references winner-bank integration and the Illusion of Novelty storytelling
layer, do not produce generic comfort copy. Start from the winner-bank mechanics,
name the familiar/stale version, identify the fresh reveal, and make the second
slide carry a specific emotional turn or physical receipt. The result should
feel like a post with a working engine, not a soft quote card.

Rejected pattern:
- "Without you everything exists, but it does not feel like home."

Repair target:
- Preserve the locked first-slide song line.
- Make slide 2 reveal the emotional backbone of the song: the ache is not that
  the speaker lacks a place, but that the speaker becomes a place waiting for
  one person to return and make it alive.
- Keep the language sendable, simple, and human.

## 2026-06-19 - Context must improve generation

Creator compared this agent-heavy repair against a plain general-LM rendition
from the same shared note and strongly preferred the general-LM version.

First misread to reject: this is not merely a preservation problem. The creator
is not saying "copy my input more closely whenever I share a shaped note."

Root cause: the studio generation layer failed to turn its accumulated learning
into better taste. With agents, winner-bank evidence, novelty modeling, copy
contracts, and prior corrections, the output should have been much better than
a plain general-LM rendition. Instead, the extra machinery made the final copy
heavier and weaker.

Active constraint: for any A Story copy generation, regardless of whether the
creator input is rough, minimal, detailed, or already emotional, the final
visible output must beat a plain general-LM baseline on emotion, clarity,
sendability, and postability. If the specialized process cannot beat that
baseline, the process has failed and must repair the generation logic, not
reframe the issue as "preserve input."

Clarification: the plain baseline is mandatory for all creative work, but it is
not a preserve-only mode and it is not an instruction to narrow the work. It is
the control sample. The creator's agent terms, winner-bank language,
storytelling language, hooks, and novelty references are style and architecture
signals meant to make the final delivery more nuanced, more robust, and more
tasteful. The thinking responsibility stays with the coordinator. Do not let
the terms replace judgment, and do not treat the architecture as a reason to
stop exploring.

Rejected pattern:
- Treating more context, more agents, or more artifacts as proof of better
  generation.
- Explaining the failure as a prompt-preservation issue when the real issue is
  weak creative synthesis.
- Letting framework knowledge make the output more constructed, less moving,
  or less immediately postable.

Repair target:
- Every extra layer must earn its place in the final words.
- Specialized A Story generation should feel like a stronger creative mind, not
  a heavier workflow.
- The comparison question is: "Did all this knowledge produce something better
  than the general LM would have made?"
- The baseline comes first, then the studio layers must expand, sharpen, and
  deepen the answer without making it heavier or narrower.
