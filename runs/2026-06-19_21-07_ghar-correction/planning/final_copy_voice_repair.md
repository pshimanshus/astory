## Rejected Pattern

The previous copy explained the idea from outside:

> Log samajhte hain ghar woh hota hai...

This is too thesis-like and not sendable.

## Selected Copy

### Slide 1

Khaali hai jo tere bina,
main woh ghar hoon tera.

### Slide 2

maine tera cup
wahi rakha hai.
roz.

pata nahi,
aadat hai shayad.

par jab tak tu nahi aata na,
woh cup bhi
khaali lagta hai.

main bhi.

tu aa jaata hai,
toh lagta hai...

haan.
ab ghar hai.

## Why This Wins

- It does not define home.
- It uses one ordinary receipt instead of a polished metaphor.
- The emotional reveal is private: the speaker was not empty in general, they
  were waiting for this one person.
- The final line lands without saying "you are my home."

## Creator Rejection After Comparison

Creator later compared this agent-heavy version with a plain general-LM
rendition from the same note and strongly preferred the plain version.

Reason: this copy is more engineered than felt, and the larger failure is that
the specialized studio workflow underperformed a plain general-LM answer. The
creator's correction is not simply "preserve my note." It is that accumulated
knowledge, agents, and references should make the generation materially better
in every case. If the output is weaker than the general baseline, the generation
layer failed regardless of how much process it followed.
