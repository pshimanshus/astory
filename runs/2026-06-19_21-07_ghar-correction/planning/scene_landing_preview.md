# Scene Landing Preview

## Slide 1

Locked song-line first slide.

Visual: quiet room, one small object proving someone is missing: spare slippers,
one mug untouched, or the door chain not fully latched.

Swipe reason: the line says "ghar"; the image makes the viewer wonder what kind
of house is waiting.

## Slide 2

Emotional job: make the reader feel that the real pain is not loneliness, but
being a place that has meaning only for one person.

Recommended line direction:

> Itna ajeeb hai na...
> ghar bhi kabhi kabhi kisi ka wait karta hai.
>
> Darwaza band hota hai,
> par poora nahi.
>
> Kyunki ek awaaz,
> ek chabi,
> ek "aa gaya"
> usse phir se ghar bana deti hai.

Payoff: someone sends it because it says "I need you here" without sounding like
a generic missing-you quote.

Flat risk: if the second slide says only "without you it does not feel like
home", it collapses back into the stale surface.
