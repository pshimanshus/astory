## First-Frame Visual

Aachu is not alone in a house. She is at a cafe table full of friends, cups, chatter, and movement. Her face is readable, unsmiling, and not joining the laughter. The space beside her is unclaimed.

## Why The First Swipe Happens

The viewer should think: why does she look alone when the frame is full?

## Mini Arc

1. Full cafe, hollow Aachu.
2. Her eyes keep going back to the entrance or the empty beside-space.
3. Even a familiar cafe corner feels empty without him.
4. He still does not appear. The same noise keeps going, and Aachu finally understands why none of it feels like ghar.

## Payoff Frame

Zuv does not come back into the frame. Aachu remains in the full cafe/friends setting, still beside the unfilled space. The payoff is not relief; it is the ache landing cleanly: the place was never the missing thing.

## Why Someone Sends It

Because it says: I can be with everyone and still not feel home without you.

## Correction If It Feels Flat
