# Repair Round

## First Draft Risk

The obvious version was:

`be with someone who makes you feel heard.`

That is correct and dead. It explains the lesson instead of creating a send.

## Repairs Applied

- Preserved the source phrase `please vent more`.
- Added the A Story contrast: `i don't want the short version`.
- Replaced abstract safety with physical receipts: phone face-down, repeated detail, apology for rambling, quiet attention.
- Kept the final payoff as a sentence someone could actually send: `no. say the rest.`
- Removed therapy words: `safe space`, `hold space`, `communication`, `emotional availability`.

## Repaired Recommendation

Title: `Please Vent More`

First-slide text:

`please vent more. i don't want the short version.`

Payoff:

`no. say the rest.`

This keeps the source winner's permission engine and adds the A Story lived scene instead of making a quote-card.
