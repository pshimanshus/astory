## Recommended Idea

Title: `Please Vent More`

Exact first-slide on-image text:

`please vent more. i don't want the short version.`

Exact first-slide text:

`please vent more. i don't want the short version.`

First-frame visual:

Aachu is mid-rant in a quiet room. One hand is still in the air because she is explaining the part she usually edits out. Zuv is turned fully toward her. His phone is face-down. He is not solving, not scrolling, not doing the patient saint face. He is just there, following the plot.

Swipe reason:

The first slide makes the viewer ask whether the post understands the shame of talking too much. The swipe promises: this is not about being heard in theory; this is about the exact moment you start cutting yourself short.

Mini arc:

1. `please vent more. i don't want the short version.`
   Scene: Aachu mid-rant; Zuv's phone face-down.
2. `tell me the unnecessary details also.`
   Scene: She gestures through the tiny part she thinks is too much.
3. `who said what. what face they made. where you went quiet.`
   Scene: He follows seriously; an untouched cup or phone proves he is not waiting for her to finish.
4. `you always say 'sorry, too much' right before the real thing comes out.`
   Scene: Aachu catches herself apologizing for talking.
5. `no. say the rest.`
   Scene: The room goes quiet for one second. The love is not a hug; it is permission to keep going.

Payoff frame:

`no. say the rest.`

Why someone sends it:

The talkative/emotional partner sends it to say, without saying it directly: "please do not make me feel annoying when I am trying to tell you the real thing." The listener can send it back as a soft promise without writing a speech.

What could make it flat/generic:

- Turning it into `be with someone who listens to you`.
- Using therapy words like `safe space`, `hold space`, `communication`, or `emotional availability`.
- Staging Zuv like a counselor or saint instead of her person.
- Making the scene a quote-card with cute couple background.

Correction if it starts feeling flat:

Return to the physical receipts: phone face-down, repeated detail, "sorry, too much," and the simple final line `no. say the rest.`

Share/comment trigger:

Flat/generic risk:

First-frame visual:

Aachu checks the door twice while Zuv notices without teasing. The detail is tiny enough that nobody else would care.
