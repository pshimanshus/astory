## Scene Landing Preview

First frame: Aachu in a mirror fixing an earring, looking at Zuv through the
reflection. It stops because he is caught, not because she is posing.

Swipe reason: each next slide reveals one more way she defeats him without
saying the direct love thing.

Payoff frame: terrace shoulder-rest, his hand frozen above her hair.

Why someone sends it: it names the familiar partner behavior without explaining
love like a quote page.

Flat risk: if the images become pretty poses, the reel dies. Every frame must
show a small action: earring glance, collar excuse, water/blanket care, frozen
hand.
