## Slide Count Decision

Chosen count: 4 slides.

Why four works: the lyric feeling naturally moves through four visible couple
beats: adaayein, bahana, shikaayat plus care, and laad surrender.

Why fewer fails: two or three slides would collapse the emotional turn and make
it look like generic admiration.

Why more dilutes: more than four slides would turn a simple reel moment into an
over-explained story.
