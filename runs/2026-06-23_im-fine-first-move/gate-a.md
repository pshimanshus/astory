## 1. The unsaid feeling (the seed)

> *"I say 'I'm fine' because I'm afraid no one will notice that I'm not. I want to be
> with someone who hears the lie and stays anyway."*

The ache underneath: **being truly seen without having to ask for it.**

## 2. Engine + origin

- **Engine C — the direct send-spec** (`Be with someone who ___`). Proven:
  `DY4tGrQCXRA` "…listens like it matters" (27.04 sh/1k · 14,180 shares);
  `DZNl2zxiRD4` caption "…explain the hard parts gently…" (1.2M reach · 10,913 saves).
- **Origin: originate from a true unsaid feeling** (origin #2), shaped by engine C —
  not a templated copy of a winner.

## 3. The turn

"I'm fine" ≠ fine. The send-spec takes the smallest everyday lie and reframes it into
the **highest standard of love**: someone who reads what you don't say. The reader
sends it to the one person who does (or the one they wish would).

## 4. The receipt (one prop, per voice law #4)

**The unasked chai.** She's gone quiet and said she's fine; *without being asked* he has
already set a small steaming cup of chai by her hand. Chai is a proven A Story receipt
(engine A's mug). The proof isn't comfort — it's **reading**: he answered the lie before
she finished telling it.

## 5. On-image copy

**Primary (my confident call — honors the locked seed):**

> Be with someone who reads the "I'm fine" you don't mean.

One integrated text block, upper-middle negative space. English send-spec, matching the
engine-C winners. The lie sits *inside* the headline (in quotes), so one block both
quotes the lie and reframes it.

**Recommended sharpen (your call at this gate):** swap the lie to romanized Hinglish —

> Be with someone who reads the "main theek hoon" you don't mean.

Why I'd consider it: voice law #2's own example is *"main theek hoon"*; a real desi
person says that, not "I'm fine," and your reel lane proves the audience reads Hinglish
instantly. Risk: marginally less universal than "I'm fine," slightly higher render care.
**Pick one before Codex renders.**

## 6. The baseline this must beat (the floor)

A tasteless, memoryless LLM asked for "an Instagram post about being understood" gives:

> *"Find someone who understands you without saying a word. Real love is being seen for
> who you truly are. 💕"* — over a soft solo portrait, pretty face, no scene, no receipt,
> no turn, no one specific to send it to.

We beat it on all four winner-vs-flop axes: **two people interacting**, **a turn**, **a
specific receipt (chai)**, **earns the send**.

## 7. Scene — 1-line visual intent

Evening living room, sofa. **Aachu** lower-middle, turned slightly away, knees drawn in,
gaze down — the posture of someone who just said "I'm fine" and didn't. **Zuv** beside
her, body angled toward her, quietly reading her face; he has wordlessly set a cup of
chai by her hand, his other hand resting gently near hers. The gap between her guarded
body and his quiet pre-emptive care **is** the story.

- Two people interacting ✔ · turn ✔ · one receipt (chai) ✔ · fresh frame (not the default
  sideways two-face close-up — an over-the-shoulder / slightly-wide intimate domestic
  frame) ✔.
